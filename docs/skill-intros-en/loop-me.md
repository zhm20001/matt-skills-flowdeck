# loop-me

> ⚠️ In progress: this skill is not final and its behaviour may change.

**loop-me is a stateful grilling session whose only output is workflow specs**. It examines your world through a **loop lens**: a **loop** is a pattern that keeps recurring in your life (a career, a week, a morning, a repeated chore), and only what is predictable is worth **delegating**; a **workflow** is the spec of a loop, written to `workflows/*.md`. The glossary: **trigger** (an event beats a timer), **checkpoint** (a human verification point, and it may be absent), **push-right** (delay a checkpoint as late as it can go — the human is asked once, and asked when everything is ready), **briefing** (what a checkpoint hands you is a decision-ready summary, not raw output).

The done criteria are harsh: **an implementer agent reads the spec without needing to ask a single question** — if there are still questions, the grilling continues. The workspace also keeps a NOTES.md recording your tools, your channels, and your own names for things, so a vague word gets ground into a canonical term on the spot.

## When to use

- You want to design one of your repeated processes into a delegable workflow.
