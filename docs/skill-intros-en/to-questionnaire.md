# to-questionnaire

**to-questionnaire handles the decisions where what blocks you is not in your head but in someone else's**: it turns one into a Markdown questionnaire for that person to fill in asynchronously, or to walk through together in a meeting. The core discipline is **grilling the sending, not grilling the questions** — it only asks about things you can always answer: who it goes to (role, expertise, relationship to you, which set the tone and how much context) and what you need back (the concrete decisions and facts you cannot settle alone); the questions inside the questionnaire then aim at the **gap** between those two.

The output is `to-questionnaire-<slug>.md`: a discovery-style questionnaire structure — purpose, one paragraph of context, how to answer (deadline and effort, stressing that partial answers and "I don't know" are both useful), sections grouped by topic, **the most important questions first** (asynchronously you may only get one round), one idea per question, an answer slot with each, a "why I am asking" note only where there is a genuine risk of misreading, and a catch-all prompt to close. The replies feed into grill-with-docs or to-spec.

## When to use

- A decision needs input from a domain expert / stakeholder before it can move.

- The opposite direction from grill-me: that one interviews you, this questionnaire interviews someone else.
