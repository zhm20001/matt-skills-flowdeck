# grilling

**grilling is the interview primitive that grill-me, grill-with-docs, triage, wayfinder and improve-codebase-architecture call again and again**. The method: map the discussion onto a **design tree** — every decision carries the child decisions that depend on it; work in **rounds**, each round asking the entire **frontier** at once (the decisions whose premises are already in place and can be asked right now), numbered question by question with a recommended answer attached, and then wait for your replies; your answers reshape the tree, the frontier widens outward, and the next round opens. **Looking up facts is the agent's job** (dispatch a subagent to inspect the environment yourself instead of troubling the user with questions), **making decisions is the user's job**. The session is over only when the frontier is empty — every branch of the design tree walked, no assumption left tacit — and nothing is acted on before the user confirms shared understanding.

## When to use

- You want a plan / decision / idea stress-tested.

- You use any "grill" trigger phrase.

- You want the interview itself, with no packaging around it.
