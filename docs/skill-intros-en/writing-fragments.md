# writing-fragments

> ⚠️ In progress: this skill is not final and its behaviour may change.

**writing-fragments is the explore phase of the writing workflow**: it widens the space of what you could write and **deliberately erects no structure** — deciding structure belongs to the exploit phase (writing-beats / writing-shape). The form is a grilling interview, and the **fragments** it talks out of you get appended to a markdown file as you go: a sharp line, a claim with one line of argument behind it, a small anecdote / snippet of code / analogy, a half-finished thought, a quotation, a cluster of observations grouped by feel, and the most valuable thing of all, the **leading word** — a compact name that can carry an entire article (tracer bullet, fog of war); name it right and the structure and the title both grow out of it.

File format: one working title at the top, then a stream of fragments separated by `---`, with no sections, no tags, and no order. **Always re-read before writing** (you may have edited it), append only, never overwrite; "delete the last one" and "sharpen this one" are first-class instructions.

## When to use

- You have something to write but have not thought it through yet — gather material first, and do not rush to build a frame.
