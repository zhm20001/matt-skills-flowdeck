# writing-beats

> ⚠️ In progress: this skill is not final and its behaviour may change.

**writing-beats is the first installment of the writing workflow's exploit phase**: exploration is over, the raw material pile is topped out, and the whole pile gets arranged into a **journey of beats** (choose-your-own-adventure style). First it settles the **premise** with you (the concepts the reader already has walking in); then each round it offers 2-3 candidate **beats** — every beat may only stand on concepts that are already grounded, and it is labelled with the new concepts it grounds and therefore the later beats those unlock — you pick one beat, it writes only that beat into the article, re-reads the file, and offers the next set of candidates, looping until the journey ends on its own.

**Grounding discipline**: a concept is grounded before anything leans on it — the reader either brought it in or met it in an earlier beat; the unit of grounding is the concept, not the word. Beat size is set by the content: anything from one sentence to several paragraphs, and a five-paragraph block with three subheadings is two beats stuck together — split it. Most raw material piles leave surplus behind, and that is normal — it is exactly the point of preparing more material than you need.

## When to use

- The raw material is ready (the output of writing-fragments) and you want to arrange it into a journey-style article.
